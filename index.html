How ordering works

  1. Build your order

    Choose a size, flavor, and date above, then copy the message that appears.

  2. Send it on Messenger

    Paste it in a message to Syrell Booc on Facebook. We reply to confirm availability, the final price, and your pickup or delivery plan.

  3. Enjoy your cake

    Your cake is packed in insulated packaging so it stays frozen on the way to your celebration.

Chocolate is the crowd favorite

We asked 46 students and young adults in Cebu which flavor they like best. Chocolate won by a wide margin, so it leads our menu, with ube, vanilla, strawberry, and mango alongside it.

Questions before you order

Can I order a custom design?

Yes. Choose the Customized Large cake (10-inch, ₱799) and describe the message or design you want in the notes box. We will confirm what we can make.

How early should I order?

As early as you can. Tell us the date you need the cake in your message and we will confirm whether we can make it.

Do you deliver?

Yes, in selected areas around Cebu. To keep your cake from melting, we may limit the delivery distance, and on very hot days we may ask you to pick up your order instead.

How should I store the cake?

Keep it in the freezer until you are ready to serve. Let it sit for a few minutes before slicing so the cut is clean.
